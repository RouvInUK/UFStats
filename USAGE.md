# ustats.pro: Sideline Usage Guide

Welcome to the ultimate sideline data tracker! This guide will walk you through setting up a game, tracking stats during the chaos of a point, and interpreting your team's analytics.

## 1. Pre-Game Setup

Before the pull happens, ensure you've told the app who is on the team and who is stepping onto the field.

### Adding your Team (Roster)
1. Navigate to the **Roster** page via the dashboard menu.
2. Type a player's name and click **Add Player**. 
*(This master roster is saved to the cloud, so you only have to do this once a season!)*

### Calling Lines (Lineup)
1. Whenever a new point is starting, hit the **Lineup** button.
2. Tap the names of the 7 exact players walking onto the pitch.
3. Click Dashboard to return to the active stat tracker.

## 2. Tracking a Game (The Dashboard)

The Dashboard is designed to be used with one hand on a mobile phone, using muscle-memory so you can keep your eyes on the game.

### Setting the Match
Type the current match into the "Match Name" text box (e.g., "Finals vs Team X"). 
*Note: Our cloud-sync means if another sideline reporter already typed this match on their phone, you can just tap the box and select the active game from the dropdown. The Scoreboard point number will automatically sync to exactly where they left off!*

### Logging Actions
The UI is a fixed 3x2 grid. **Always tap a player's name first**, followed by their action.

* **Pass**: The player threw a successful pass to a teammate.
* **Point**: The player caught the disc in the endzone, scoring a point. 
  *(The app is smart: if you click Point, the engine will automatically hunt down whoever threw the last `Pass` and grant them an **Assist** too!)*
* **Throwaway**: The player threw the disc out of bounds or into the ground.
* **Drop**: The player dropped a perfectly catchable pass.
* **Stall Out**: The player was stalled while holding the disc.
* **Defence**: The player got a block or snatched an interception.
  *(Callahan Rule: If someone gets a `Defence` and immediately scores, simply click their name and click `Point`. They will get +1 Goal and +1 Block automatically!)*

### Archiving a Match
When the game is over, click the **✓ Close Game** button next to the match name. This archives the game so it doesn't clutter everyone's "Active games" dropdown next week.

## 3. Reviewing the Tape (Analytics)

Click the **📊 Stats** button to view your advanced metrics.
- You can view "All" data at once or filter by a specific match.
- **Comp %**: Evaluates the safety of your handlers `(Total Completions / Total Pass Attempts)`. Throwaways, Drops, and Stalls immediately hurt this.
- **Turnovers**: The red number is the total errors made by a player. The small letters next to it break the turnovers down specifically into `(T)`hrowaways, `(D)`rops, and `(S)`tall Outs so you can analyze where the offensive breakdowns are happening.
